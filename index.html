What is Tamble?

Tamble is a retro pixel-art, top-down tactical RPG you play with friends. Form a party of up to six, descend into the dungeon, and take it on one turn at a time: positioning, combos, puzzles, and enemies that are smarter than they look.

Every floor down is harder than the last, and the loot gets better to match. Level up, grow through more than 55 classes, and build your character exactly the way you want to play.

Tactics with your whole crew

Clear entire dungeons together in turn-based battles where every move counts. Plan your positions, cover each other, and outsmart dastardly enemy AI on the way to epic boss fights.

55+ classes to grow into

Level up your characters and move through more than 55 classes. Mix and match skills and item loadouts until your build plays exactly how you want it to.

The deeper you go, the better it gets

Each level down brings tougher challenges, nastier enemies and better and better loot. Push your luck, or head back up with what you’ve found.

  • One-of-a-kind loot

    The rarest drops are serialized items that live in your Steam inventory. Nobody else has yours.

  • Achievements and titles

    Earn achievements and unlock titles that show everyone where you’ve been.

  • Party vs. party Planned

    Down the road: take your party up against other players in live turn-based battles.
